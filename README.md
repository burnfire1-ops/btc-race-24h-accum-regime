ACCUM-A + regime if-else is a long-only accumulation bot for the make.fun BTC Race 24h competition (btc-race-24h-20261118-1444, cbBTC vs USDC, 1-minute bars, $1,000 paper). It uses the strict ACCUM-A entry (75 bps flush below the 90-minute SMA with 15-minute RSI(2) under 20, or at least two of four 15-minute mean-reversion legs while 15-minute ATR is under 20 bps of price), except when the market is in an extreme oversold regime (RSI(2) of the just-closed 15-minute bar under 10), in which case it uses the looser entry (50 bps flush with RSI(2) under 30, or at least one of the four legs), buying 25% of capital ($250) per tranche and adding another 25% each time price drops 1% from the last fill (max 4 tranches), and exits on a 3% take-profit, a stop, a trailing stop that arms at +2%, or a 4-hour time stop counted from the first buy, all computed from the qty-weighted average entry. On this competition's frozen 24-hour dev window (a falling market, -2.80%) it made 1 trade for a net PnL of +$0.46, so it passes the dev gate (PnL > $0); the strict ACCUM-A entry alone made 0 trades ($0.00) and the loose entry alone lost -$0.52 on the same window.
